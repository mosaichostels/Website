Dormitory Beds at Mosaic Hostel

Mosaic Hostel is a backpacker hostel 100 m from Assi Ghat in Varanasi. We offer dormitory beds in five dorms: a 4-bed, a 6-bed, an 8-bed and a 10-bed mixed dorm, and a 6-bed female-only dorm. Every dorm has air conditioning, free WiFi and lockers, and the hostel has a shared kitchen, hot-water showers, a rooftop and a 24-hour reception.

Guests rate us 4.3 out of 5 on average across booking platforms (394 reviews).

Frequently Asked Questions

How far are the dorms from Assi Ghat?

Mosaic Hostel is 100 m from Assi Ghat, so every dorm is a short walk from the ghat steps.

Do you have female-only dorms?

Yes. Mosaic Hostel has a 6-bed female-only dorm alongside the mixed dorms, with the same amenities.

What is included with a dorm bed?

Every dorm has air conditioning, free WiFi and lockers, and guests can use the hot-water showers, shared kitchen and rooftop. Reception is open 24 hours.

Can I rent a locker?

Yes. Lockers are available for rent, or you can bring your own lock. Ask at reception.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 13:00 and check-out is by 10:30. Reception is open 24 hours.

Are overnight guests allowed in the dorms?

No. Overnight guests are not permitted in dorm rooms.
