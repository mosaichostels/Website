Dorms at Mosaic Hostel

Mosaic Hostel is a backpacker hostel 100 m from Assi Ghat in Varanasi. We have four dorms: a 4-bed, a 6-bed and an 8-bed mixed dorm, and a 6-bed female-only dorm. Every dorm has air conditioning, free WiFi and lockers, and the hostel has a shared kitchen, hot-water showers, a rooftop and a 24-hour reception. Guests rate us 4.8 from 427 reviews.

What Every Dorm Bed Includes

  • Air conditioning and free high-speed WiFi
  • Lockers. Bring your own lock or rent one at reception
  • Hot-water showers and shared bathrooms
  • A shared kitchen and a rooftop space
  • 24-hour reception. Check-in from 13:00, check-out by 10:30

Overnight guests are not permitted in the dorms.

Location: 100 m From Assi Ghat

The hostel is at B1/85C, Assi Ghat Road, Anandbagh, Varanasi, 100 m from Assi Ghat, where the sunrise and the evening aarti happen. Our Assi Ghat guide covers the ghat, and our transfer guide shows how to get here from the station and the airport.

Frequently Asked Questions

How far are the dorms from Assi Ghat?

Mosaic Hostel is 100 m from Assi Ghat, so every dorm is a short walk from the ghat steps.

Do you have female-only dorms?

Yes. Mosaic Hostel has a 6-bed female-only dorm alongside the mixed dorms, with the same amenities.

What is included with a dorm bed?

Every dorm has air conditioning, free WiFi and lockers, and guests can use the hot-water showers, shared kitchen and rooftop. Reception is open 24 hours.

Can I rent a locker?

Yes. Lockers are available for rent, or you can bring your own lock. Ask at reception.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 13:00 and check-out is by 10:30. Reception is open 24 hours.

Are overnight guests allowed in the dorms?

No. Overnight guests are not permitted in dorm rooms.
