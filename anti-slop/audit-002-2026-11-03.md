# antislop audit 002, 2026-11-03 (AFTER mode, read-only)

Method: taste-skill `lint.mjs` on the local source (35 files) and `inspect.mjs` on the LIVE site at 1440 / 768 / 390 px for 10 pages (home, about, dorms, gallery, contact, book-now, privacy, blog index, and two posts: Assi Ghat guide, best hostels). I opened 4 screenshots (home desktop and mobile, dorms desktop, book-now mobile); the rest are judged from the inspector's measurements. The other 14 blog posts share the post template and were not inspected one by one. Impeccable's detector was not run: its `context` loaded PRODUCT.md, and the design critique below is my own reading of the screenshots. Priority follows the antislop tier (Hard Gate = HIGH, Purpose-Gate = MEDIUM, Quality Locks = LOW).

Direction exists (DESIGN.md: Cinzel + Cormorant + Jost, gold/cream/ink, a coloured "tile" stripe motif). Per R-37 a stylistic choice that the direction owns is identity, not slop; items marked **owner call** collide with a named rule and need keep-or-drop from you. Dials as built: ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2.

## HIGH

1. **Reduced-motion leaves content invisible.** (R-39) With `prefers-reduced-motion` on, 3 "Read More" links on `/blog/` and 6 category labels on `/gallery` (Rooms, Private Room Bed, Common Areas, Interior Design) stay hidden on the first screen. Defect, not taste.
2. **Text covered at some scroll positions on phones.** (R-03) At 390 px the inspector found large text lying under another element: home ("Common Room", "Why Backpackers Choose Mosaic", "Begin Your Mosaic Story"), about, dorms ("8-bed mixed dorm"), and both posts inspected. Could be the sticky Book Now / WhatsApp bar or a layout overlap, so it needs a manual check on a phone before any fix.
3. **Tap targets below 44 px.** (R-03) Inline links 18 to 24 px high, "WhatsApp us" on book-now 18 px, email link on contact 19 px, header phone link and "← Home / Blog →" 42 px, desktop nav 22 px. Carries over from today's SEO visual audit and audit-001.

## MEDIUM

4. **Small text.** 34 text blocks under 13 px on the home page; running text is 12 px on `/contact` and 14 px on `/dorms/` and `/gallery` (the dorm cards' amenity and "suits" lines are visibly tiny in the screenshot). Readability and accessibility.
5. **Headlines run past two lines.** Home H1 "Budget Hostel in Varanasi near Assi Ghat" is 3 lines at 1440 and at 390; "A Backpacker Hostel at Assi Ghat, Varanasi" is 4 lines; "Why Backpackers Choose Mosaic in Varanasi" is 3 on a phone (6 pages total). The H1 wording is SEO-owned, so shorten it only with your say-so, or widen the measure.
6. **Uneven page margins.** 80 px left vs 128 to 305 px right at 1440 px on about, blog index, both posts, book-now and privacy; 32 vs 80 px on dorms; 20 vs 93 px on contact at 768. Content hugs the left with an empty right side.
7. **Eyebrow labels and tiny tracked uppercase labels.** (R-06, R-09; **owner call**) 12 eyebrows and 20 micro-labels on the home page alone ("Where Culture Meets Comfort", "Our Spaces", "Scroll"), present on all 10 pages. They are part of the current brand voice.
8. **Gradients and shadows.** (R-01, R-12) The hero and room-card overlays are legitimate scrims over photos; the progress bar (`#progress`), `site.js:110`, `gallery.html:491-492` and `global.css:130` use decorative gradients, plus 5 text-shadows on the home hero and a box-shadow at `global.css:12`. Keep the scrims, justify or drop the rest.
9. **Few pictures.** `/book-now`, `/contact`, `/privacy` and `/blog/` have no photographs (blog cards have none), `/about` and `/dorms/` have 3. The first screen of every inner page has no dominant element: the largest type is 64 px over a dark title band.
10. **Placeholders where room photos should be.** (R-23) The 10-bed and 6-bed female dorm cards show a coloured tile graphic instead of a room photo. Honest placeholder, but it needs real photos from you.
11. **One blank stretch.** `/gallery` at 390 px has a 524 px empty run.

## LOW

12. **Hairlines and outlines.** 38 `<hr>` dividers in the blog posts, 15 1px outlined boxes (home `index.html:366-371` and others), 9 thin rules on the home page. Mostly structure in the posts; owner call.
13. **Emoji / glyphs.** `♥` in footers (34 hits) and `★` in the rating text. The star next to "4.3" carries meaning; the hearts are decoration.
14. **Type system.** 3 typefaces (Jost, Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel), 15 distinct font sizes on the home page, cream grounds (`#faf4ea`, `#f2e8d5`), and soft-grey body text at 6.1:1 (passes AA, reads thin). Brand identity; the size count is the cleanup candidate.
15. **Stock phrase.** "More than a ..." appears 4 times (home, three posts).
16. **Book-now stepper numbering (1 Dates, 2 Rooms ...).** Defended: it is a real progress indicator, not decoration.

## Checks that came back clean
No horizontal overflow on any inspected page at the three widths; hero video is the first-screen media; no fake testimonials or statistics (the 4.3 / 394 rating is labelled as a cross-platform average and was owner-approved); no em dash in visible text (one in an HTML comment); the viewport meta allows zoom.
