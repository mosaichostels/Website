# Audit 003, 2026-11-04: footer (all 25 pages)

Scope: the `<footer>` block, identical on all 25 pages (one hash across `index`, `404`, `about`, `book-now`, `contact`, `gallery`, `privacy`, `dorms/`, `blog/` and the 16 posts). Mode: antislop AFTER (session override), plus impeccable audit and ui-ux-pro-max checks. Read-only: nothing was changed by this audit.

Evidence: live site at 1440 px and 390 px (computed styles, tap sizes, contrast by blending each colour over `#1a1208`, focus state, overflow), screenshots of `/about` footer, local source. Not run: the impeccable detector CLI, a screen reader, the Google "Add as Preferred Source" button (it is not live yet, see #1).

## Verified OK

- Contrast (blended over `#1a1208`): body and links 7.13:1, gold headings 6.06:1, phone number 9.85:1. All pass AA.
- Focus: every footer link shows a 2 px outline plus a gold 5 px ring, visible on the dark ground.
- Phone: every link is 44 px tall, no horizontal overflow, the fixed Book Now / WhatsApp bar clears the last line.
- Phone number is a real `tel:` link with a 44 px hit area.
- Nothing fabricated: no fake stats, testimonials or badges in the footer (R-17, R-18, R-36).
- No glow, glass, shadow or gradient in the footer (R-10 to R-13 pass).

## Findings

1. **[MED, R-24 / R-26, product principle 1] No booking link in the footer.** Explore lists Dorm Beds, Gallery, About, Blog, Contact, Privacy; Connect lists Instagram, WhatsApp, Email. There is no Book Now. On a site whose success is a direct booking, the last thing a reader sees should offer it. (Home and Dorms are also absent from Explore.)
2. **[MED, R-39 / C-4] The Preferred Source button is unvalidated.** It sits inside Connect, a column of contact channels, and the button is not a contact channel. Google draws it, so its size, colours, contrast on `#1a1208` and tap target are not under the site's control and have not been seen: locally the container renders empty at 40 px high, and Google may draw nothing on the real domain either. Check it live after deploy at 390 and 1440 px; if it is a light button, give it its own row under the links. It also adds a Google request on every page; `privacy.html` mentions Google embeds in general terms, not this script.
3. **[MED, R-39] Link groups have no semantics.** "Explore" and "Connect" are `div.footer-heading`, the links are bare `<a>` in a `div`. A screen reader hears an unlabelled run of links. Make the headings real headings (or give the groups `aria-label`), and wrap each group in `nav` with a list, or use `ul`. The footer is a `contentinfo` landmark already.
4. **[MED, R-05 / C-1] Footer copied into 25 files.** The comment on every page says "FOOTER (injected by footer.js)" but `footer.js` only paints the 5-colour strip; the markup is static. Any footer change is a 25-file edit (the button just was). Either inject the footer from `footer.js` (SEO risk: links must stay in static HTML for crawlers, so no) or keep static and fix the comment, and add a one-line drift check (all footers hash identical) to `live_check` or `verify.sh`.
5. **[LOW, R-03 / R-39] Desktop links are 19 px tall.** Phone gets 44 px (global.css `@media`), desktop keeps 13 px type at 19 px. It passes WCAG 2.2 target-size by spacing (27 px pitch), not by size. Raising desktop padding to 24 px or more also helps pointer users.
6. **[LOW, R-06] 13 px type for links and legal lines.** Readable at 7.13:1 but small; the body copy elsewhere is 15 px. 14 px would match the tagline.
7. **[LOW, R-20] The spacer `<div></div>`.** The 4-column grid has an empty second column, so the footer reads as logo block on the left, then a gap of about 400 px, then two short columns. It is a layout hack, and the right half of the footer is sparse. Use a 3-column grid (`1.4fr 1fr 1fr`) and drop the empty div.
8. **[LOW, visual] The tile strip is off-centre.** `.footer-bottom` is `space-between` with unequal side texts (252 px and 157 px), so the strip centres at x=768 on a 1440 px page, not 720. Give both side items equal flex-basis or centre the strip absolutely.
9. **[LOW, R-39] Inline styles and a recolour filter on the logo.** `style="…filter:brightness(0) invert(1);opacity:0.9"` on the `<img>` and `style="font-size:13px…"` on the address paragraph, repeated 25 times and patched by an `!important` rule (`footer p[style*="opacity"]`). Move them into the `footer` rules in `global.css`.
10. **[LOW] Address is not a link.** The address in the footer is bold text; every other page links it to Google Maps. Link it the same way (`target="_blank" rel="noopener"`).
11. **[LOW, R-39] New-tab links give no warning.** Instagram and WhatsApp use `target="_blank"` with no `rel` and no hint. Modern browsers imply `noopener`, so this is accessibility only: add `rel="noopener"` and `aria-label="Instagram (opens in a new tab)"` style text, or accept as is.
12. **[LOW, R-16 / copy] Generic lines.** "A hostel rooted in community, warmth, and the spirit of Varanasi. Every guest who stays becomes part of our story." and "© 2026 Mosaic Hostel. All rights reserved." say nothing that is specific to the place. Candidates the owner can confirm: the 100 m to Assi Ghat, check-in from 1 PM and 24-hour reception, free cancellation up to 72 hours. Copy is the owner's call (SEO wording is locked unless approved).
13. **[LOW] No cancellation or house-rules link.** The only policy link is Privacy. The booking policies live at `/book-now#faq-heading`. A "Cancellation and house rules" link would answer the most common pre-booking question from any page.

## Suggested order

1. #1 and #13 together (one new row of links), 2. #3 and #7 (semantics and grid, one footer rewrite), 3. #2 after deploy (look at the real button), 4. #4 (drift check) with #9 (inline styles to CSS), 5. #5, #6, #8, #10, #11, #12 as taste items.
