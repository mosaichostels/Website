## Findings

### Backend and payments
1. **[MED, P1] Cancel is allowed for in-house and other post-arrival statuses.** `api/cancel-booking.php:55` only refuses Cancel/Void/Checked Out; `components/book-now.js:1185,1190` mirrors it. A guest with reservation number + email can cancel a "Checked In" stay (no refund inside 72 h, but the booking is released). Fix: allow only pre-arrival statuses. The eZee status words must be probed first, not guessed.
2. **[MED, P2] `availability.php` has no site-wide ceiling.** Only the per-client limit (`api/availability.php:13`, keyed on spoofable X-Forwarded-For). create-order/lookup/cancel each have a site-wide cap; availability does not, and every call is a live eZee query. Conversely the site-wide `create-order-all` cap (60/10 min) can be exhausted by one spoofing client to block all bookings. Documented trade-off in `config.php:99`; real fix is at the WAF.
3. **[LOW, P3] Stranger can lock a booking's lookup/cancel for an hour.** `rate_limit('cancel-reservation', 5, 3600, $reservationNo)` runs before the email check (`cancel-booking.php:36`, same in lookup). Five wrong-email tries lock the real guest out; they WhatsApp. Accept or count only failed attempts.
4. **[LOW, P3] `cancel-booking.php:54` does not normalise a single-object `BookingTran`** the way `lookup-booking.php:44` does. A bare-object reply reads status '' and skips the already-cancelled guard.
5. **[LOW, P3] `alert-email.php` answers 200 (empty) from the web.** Harmless constant file; block with the same rule as `api/lib/`.
6. **[MED, P2] Live PHP versus `main` is unverifiable from here.** `deploy-drift.sh` compares pages only. STATUS says the ProcessBooking room-assignment change was "edited locally, NOT committed or deployed", yet it is committed (`ede5071`); whether `api/lib/booking.php` on the host has it is unknown. Needs an FTP read-back (needs your go-ahead). Also known and unfixed: the cron recovery path does not repeat room assignment (`booking.php:198` shortcut).
7. **[LOW, P3] Test gaps** (already in TESTING): no automated test for `components/*.js`, `.htaccess` rules or CI. Backend is well covered (below).

### Accessibility and interaction
8. **[HIGH, P1, R-39/R-32] `cursor:none` on `body` and about 25 interactive classes** (`styles/global.css`: buttons, `.field-check`, `.qty-btn`, `.cal-day`, `.room-option`, lightbox...). The replacement cursor is two divs moved by `site.js`. If `site.js` is blocked or fails, desktop users have no cursor at all on the booking page; even when it works, pointer-affordance is lost. Fix: drop `cursor:none`, keep `cursor:pointer` on controls; the decorative dot may stay.
9. **[HIGH, P2, R-39/R-19] Reduced motion is only half honoured.** CSS shortens durations (`global.css` reduced-motion block), but magnetic buttons, ripple, card tilt and the scroll bar (`site.js:123-150`, `book-now.js:73-85`) still move with the pointer. Gate them in JS with `matchMedia('(prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)')`.
10. **[HIGH, P2, R-32] Calendar popup is unreachable by Tab.** It is appended to the end of `<body>` and focus never moves into it (`book-now.js:1074-1085`); day cells are `div role=button` with no arrow-key grid. Typing YYYY-MM-DD is the documented fallback. Fix: move focus into the popup on open (and return it on close), or use native `<input type=date>` on touch.
11. **[HIGH, P2, R-03] Touch targets under 44 px.** Booking: `.cal-nav` 26x26 (the `.booking-widget .qty-btn` buttons were already 44 px, so the 28 px reading was wrong), gallery filter buttons 40 px high. Standalone links: "WhatsApp us" 66x18 (book-now), "See our spaces" 89x20 (about), contact address/review/Instagram links 19-38 px. Inline blog links (23 px) are exempt under WCAG 2.5.8 but short.
12. **[MED, P2] Maps link uses `confirm("OK = Google Maps / Cancel = Apple Maps")`** (`site.js:158-164`), and `maps://` does nothing off Apple devices. Replace with two plain links.
13. **[LOW, P3] Native `window.confirm` for cancel** (`book-now.js:1205`) and a text date field with `inputmode="none"`: works, but unstyled and typed-ISO dates are awkward.
14. **[LOW, P3] Contrast not conclusively measured.** My automated sweep could not resolve the transparent nav over dark bands (false positives, discarded). Screenshots of nav, hero and form labels looked fine, but no AA pass/fail is claimed. WCAG AA is still "undecided" in DECISIONS.

### Copy, trust and claims
15. **[HIGH, R-02] Em dashes in live text.** 358 `—` in 25 HTML files (counts include comments/JSON-LD), 43 in `book-now.js`, and in API messages (`create-order.php` 17, `booking.php` 13...). Confirmed in live titles, e.g. "Book Mosaic Hostel Varanasi Direct — Dorms & Rooms", "Photo Gallery: Dorms, Rooms & Rooftop — Mosaic Hostel", and body copy on /about. DESIGN.md has no voice rule asking for them. Fix: replace in text; leave code comments alone (exempt as non-UI).
16. **[HIGH, R-17/R-36, needs owner decision] Rating claim vs Google.** Site shows "4.8 ★ guest rating · 427 reviews" (hero, stats, book-now, Hostel JSON-LD `aggregateRating`). Google Business Profile read today: 4.6 average over 86 reviews. PRODUCT.md records 4.8/427 as an owner-confirmed cross-platform figure, but the page never says "across Hostelworld, Booking.com and Google", and self-serving `aggregateRating` on your own site is not eligible for Google review rich results. Per R-37 I am not changing it silently: either label the sources, or show the Google figure, or drop the JSON-LD rating.
