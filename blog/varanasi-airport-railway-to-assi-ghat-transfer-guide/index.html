Varanasi Airport & Railway Station to Assi Ghat: Transfer Guide

Trip Planning
Airport & Railway Transfer Guide
How to get from the airport or station to Assi Ghat
Published: 2026-07-29 | Author: Mosaic Hostel Team

Summary

How to get from Varanasi's airport or railway station to Assi Ghat — routes, timing, realistic pricing, and what to actually expect when you arrive, whether you're flying in or coming by train.

Arriving in a new city late at night or after a long journey is when you're most likely to get overcharged or confused about logistics. Here's exactly what to expect getting from Varanasi's two main arrival points to Assi Ghat.

From Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport (Babatpur)

Mosaic Hostel is about 25–26 km from Varanasi's airport (Lal Bahadur Shastri, Babatpur), and the journey by road typically takes 45 minutes to an hour, depending on traffic and time of day — night arrivals are usually faster.

Pre-paid taxi: The airport has an official pre-paid taxi counter inside the arrivals area. This is the most reliable option for a first-time visitor — you pay a fixed, posted rate at the counter before you get in the car, which removes any negotiation entirely. Expect to pay somewhere in the ₹700–₹1,000 range for a private car to Assi Ghat, though rates shift with fuel prices and season.

App-based cabs (Ola/Uber): Both operate at the airport, though availability can be inconsistent late at night. Pricing is usually comparable to or slightly cheaper than the pre-paid counter, and you get the advantage of a fixed, transparent fare shown before you book.

Avoid: touts approaching you before you reach the official counter, especially outside the terminal. If someone offers you a ride before you've reached the pre-paid taxi desk or an app, politely decline.

From Varanasi Junction (Varanasi Cantt) Railway Station

This is the main railway station most travelers arrive at, and it's considerably closer — Mosaic Hostel is about 7–8 km from Varanasi Junction, a 20 to 30 minute ride depending on traffic through the old city's narrower approach roads.

Auto-rickshaw: The most common and most economical option. Expect to pay roughly ₹150–₹250 for the ride, though prices are almost always negotiable and can spike late at night or during peak festival periods (see our seasonal guide for exactly when). Agree on the price before getting in.

App-based cabs: Reliable in Varanasi and a good option if you'd rather not negotiate — Ola in particular has decent coverage around the station.

Cycle-rickshaw: Slower but cheap, and can be a genuinely pleasant way to see the old city if you're not in a hurry and it's daylight. Not recommended with heavy luggage or late at night.

Practical Tips for Either Arrival Point

Have your accommodation's address written down or saved offline — B1/85C, Assi Ghat Road, Anandbagh is specific enough that most drivers will recognize the area, but exact house numbers in the old city can be hard to find without a landmark. Mentioning "Assi Ghat" as the destination is usually enough to get you close.

Arriving very late at night (after 11 PM) is manageable at both the airport and the station — both have taxi availability round the clock — but expect a modest price premium and consider messaging ahead so your accommodation knows your arrival time.

SIM cards and data are worth sorting at the airport if you land during the day (several counters sell tourist SIMs), since having maps and messaging working from the moment you land makes the whole transfer smoother.

Ask Your Hostel to Arrange Pickup

Most hostels, including Mosaic, can arrange a fair-rate pickup if you message ahead with your flight or train details — this is often the least stressful option for a first arrival, since it removes any negotiation entirely and means someone is actually looking for you at arrivals.
