Dev Deepawali in Varanasi 2026: Date, Ghats & Planning Tips

Festival Guide
Dev Deepawali 2026
When it is, what to expect on the ghats, and how to plan
Published: 2026-10-14 | Updated: 2026-10-18 | Author: Mosaic Hostel Team

Summary

Dev Deepawali, the festival of lights of the gods, falls on Kartik Purnima. In 2026 that is 24 November. The ghats are lit with earthen lamps and the city is at its busiest, so plan early.

When Is Dev Deepawali 2026?

Dev Deepawali is celebrated on Kartik Purnima, the full moon of the Hindu month of Kartik, about fifteen days after Diwali. In 2026 most calendars give 24 November. Some sources list the following day, so check a current panchang closer to the date.

What Happens in Varanasi

Tradition holds that on this night the gods come down to Kashi to bathe in the Ganga. In the evening the ghats along the river are lined with earthen lamps, and the Ganga Aarti at Dashashwamedh Ghat becomes a larger, more elaborate ceremony. Two rituals mark the day: a holy bath in the Ganga during the month of Kartik (kartik snan) and, in the evening, the offering of lit lamps to the river (deepdan). Boats carry visitors along the river to see the ghats lit up from the water. Our guide to the Ganga Aarti at Assi Ghat and Dashashwamedh explains the regular ceremony.

Where to Watch from the Ghats

The lamps run along the whole riverfront, from Ravidas Ghat at the southern end to Rajghat in the north, so there is no single best spot. It depends on how much crowd you want. In the south, Assi Ghat has its own evening aarti and is the calmer place to start. About 3 km north, roughly 15 to 20 minutes by road on a normal evening, is Dashashwamedh Ghat, the centre of the festival, where the Ganga Aarti is larger than usual and the steps fill up early. Further north are Manikarnika, Panchganga and Rajghat. If crowds worry you, begin at Assi Ghat at dusk and decide from there whether to go on. A boat gives the widest view, because you see the lit ghats in one sweep from the water.

Plan Early

  • Stay: beds in Varanasi fill up around the festival. Book as early as you can.
  • Boats: demand is high, so arrange your boat in advance and agree the price and the pick-up point first.
  • Crowds: the main ghats are very crowded after sunset. Go early, stay with your group and keep valuables secure.
  • Getting around: roads near the central ghats can be closed or congested. Expect to walk part of the way.
  • Weather: late November is cool in the evening. See best time to visit Varanasi.

Where to Stay

Many travelers prefer a base at Assi Ghat, where the evening is calmer, and travel to the central ghats for the main lamp-lighting. Our Assi Ghat vs Dashashwamedh comparison helps you choose, and dorm vs private room helps you pick a bed. Check our transfer guide for arrival day.

Details such as timings, closures and the final date are set locally each year, so confirm them close to the festival.

Dev Deepawali FAQ

When is Dev Deepawali in 2026?

It falls on Kartik Purnima, the full moon of the month of Kartik, which most calendars place on 24 November 2026. Some sources list the following day, so check a current panchang closer to the date.

What is Dev Deepawali?

Dev Deepawali means the Diwali of the gods. Tradition holds that the gods come down to Kashi to bathe in the Ganga on this night. The ghats are lit with earthen lamps and devotees offer lit lamps to the river.

Which ghats are lit up for Dev Deepawali?

The steps of the ghats along the riverfront, from Ravidas Ghat in the south to Rajghat in the north, are lit with earthen lamps. Dashashwamedh Ghat is the centre of the celebrations.

Do I need to book a boat in advance?

Yes. Demand for boats is high on the night, so arrange yours ahead and agree the price and the pick-up point first.

How far is Assi Ghat from Dashashwamedh Ghat?

About 3 km, roughly 15 to 20 minutes by road on a normal evening. On the festival night, roads near the central ghats can be closed or congested, so expect to walk part of the way.
