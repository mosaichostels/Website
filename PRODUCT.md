# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

International backpackers and solo travellers (including solo women) and domestic Indian travellers (friends, groups, couples), treated as equal audiences. They research Varanasi stays through Google and the blog, compare hostels, and book direct on the site or via WhatsApp, mostly on mobile.

## Product Purpose

Direct-booking website for Mosaic Hostel Varanasi (est. 2025, near Assi Ghat). Success is a completed direct booking (eZee availability, Razorpay payment) or a WhatsApp enquiry, reached without an OTA.

## Positioning

Kept as already set by the SEO work and live site copy: "Premium budget hostel near Assi Ghat" / "Budget Hostel in Varanasi near Assi Ghat", with dorm beds and private rooms. Supporting claims already on the site: steps from Assi Ghat, female-only dorms, book direct and skip OTA commissions, a team that lives on site.

## Operating Context

Check-in from 1:00 PM, check-out by 10:30 AM, 24-hour reception. Free cancellation up to 72 hours before check-in, charged in full inside 72 hours. Valid government photo ID required from every guest. WhatsApp +91-9125492225 is the fastest channel.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static HTML/CSS/JS with no build step, plus PHP endpoints in `api/` (eZee PMS, Razorpay); deployed by FTP to Hostinger. Assumed to continue; not yet confirmed as binding.
- Pages: home, about, gallery, book-now, contact, privacy, blog (15 travel guides).
- Open decision: dorm lineup is inconsistent. Homepage lists an 8-bed mixed dorm and "5 room types"; `llms.txt` lists only 6-bed mixed plus 6-bed and 4-bed female-only dorms.
- Open decision: whether WCAG AA is a formal requirement.

## Brand Commitments

Name "Mosaic Hostel Varanasi" and the tile idea ("Each guest is a piece. Each story is a tile.") are in use across copy and the hero. Existing look uses a warm cream and gold palette with Cinzel, Cormorant Garamond and Jost; recorded as incumbent only, not yet declared binding.

## Evidence on Hand

21 gallery photos, hero video and poster, OTA/Hostelworld logos (`images/`), 15 blog guides. Site claims "1500+ guests", "4.8 ★ (427 reviews)" and "60+ countries"; these are owner-supplied and not verifiable from the repo. Future work must not invent further reviews, ratings, prices or counts.

## Product Principles

1. Direct booking is the primary conversion; every page should shorten the path to it.
2. Serve international and domestic guests equally; WhatsApp stays a first-class channel.
3. Trust and safety (female-only dorms, 24h reception, clear cancellation and ID rules) are stated plainly.
4. Only use proof the owner can stand behind.
5. SEO-established positioning and copy structure are preserved unless the owner says otherwise.
