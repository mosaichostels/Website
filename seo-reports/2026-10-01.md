## 12. Fix-all pass (owner granted permission for api/ and booking JS)

Codex review: round 1 objection (an eZee reply with no `ReservationNo`, or JSON on an HTTP 5xx, was treated as a definite rejection and could double-book on retry) fixed; final verdict OK.

**API (`api/`):** confirm step validates order/payment ids and checks Razorpay amount, currency and status (webhook and cron); a late payment on an abandoned order is still claimed; only an *explicit* eZee rejection is retried (max 3, via `pending/`), while any timeout, 5xx or reservation-less reply goes to `failed/` with an alert that says to check eZee before re-queueing; stale `processing/` orders are parked in `failed/` with an alert (never auto-requeued); the webhook returns 500 on failed/retry so Razorpay redelivers; `verify-payment` requires POST, type-checks ids and refuses an empty secret; cancel has per-reservation and site-wide guess limits, id validation, and writes a refund to-do plus an alert (no automatic refund, a money decision); create-order type-checks every field and sums duplicate cart lines against availability; availability is GET-only with string checks (the array-param 500 is gone); log lines are stripped of control characters and the APIKey; `api/lib` and `secrets.php` are denied over HTTP. New optional `ALERT_EMAIL` in `secrets.php` (see `secrets.example.php`); without it alerts are logged only.

**Front end:** friendly errors for non-JSON/failed responses with timeouts, scroll to each new stage, IST "today", case-insensitive trimmed email confirm, `begin_checkout` fires once per selection, new `payment_failed` / `payment_dismissed` events, no second Razorpay window while one is open, retry button hidden once paid, stale results cleared so Back cannot reopen them, cancel asks for confirmation, calendar arrows labelled, policy modal is a dialog, `autocomplete` and `inputmode` on fields, 16px controls on phones, success-text and step-label contrast.
