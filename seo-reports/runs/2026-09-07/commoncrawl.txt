## Inbound links — what Common Crawl can and cannot tell you
  The CDX index is keyed by crawled URL, never by link target: there is no
  backlink query, and any tool claiming otherwise is using the webgraph.
  Run again with --webgraph for the one CC dataset that does answer it
  (quarterly domain-vertices, ~900 MB gzip, streamed with early exit,
  verdict cached permanently).
