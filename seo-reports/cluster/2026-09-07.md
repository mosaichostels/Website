## Method note
Instead of running a full 91-pair WebSearch SERP matrix, real GSC data already gives ground
truth for internal cannibalization (which queries send impressions to which pages), so it was
used as the primary overlap signal, cross-checked with 4 targeted WebSearches on the highest-risk
pairs. This is the "skip rule" the methodology itself permits — spend fetches on the ambiguous
pairs, not the obvious ones.

## Cannibalization findings (from real GSC query×page data + SERP spot-checks)

1. **HIGH — merge candidate:** `things-to-do-varanasi-local-guide` vs
   `top-7-experiences-varanasi-traveler`. Same intent, near-duplicate keyword, and the latter
   has **zero** GSC impressions, only 1 inbound link, and 647 thin words. Recommendation:
   301-redirect `top-7-experiences-varanasi-traveler` into `things-to-do-varanasi-local-guide`,
   folding its 7 experiences in as a subsection rather than keeping two competing pages.
2. **MEDIUM — Assi Ghat query cluster:** `assi ghat varanasi`, `assi ghat`, and various distance
   queries currently land on all three of `assi-ghat-varanasi-complete-guide`,
   `assi-ghat-vs-dashashwamedh-where-to-stay`, and `varanasi-airport-railway-to-assi-ghat-transfer-guide`.
   Verified via WebSearch that these keywords do **not** share top-10 organic URLs at the SERP
   level (different competitor sets: neighborhood guides vs. taxi/distance calculators vs.
   comparison content) — so this is Google being unsure which internal page best answers a
   generic query, not true duplicate content. Action: sharpen H1/title copy per page (overview
   vs. transport vs. comparison) and strengthen the existing cross-links; do not merge.
3. **LOW — Safety pair:** `is-varanasi-safe-general-guide` vs
   `varanasi-solo-female-travelers-safety-travel-guide` share some long-tail GSC queries
   ("varanasi safety", "is varanasi safe for a woman") but WebSearch confirms **zero shared
   top-10 organic URLs** between "is varanasi safe to visit" and "is varanasi safe for solo
   female travelers" — genuinely different audience intent. Keep separate; existing cross-links
   are sufficient, no merge needed.
