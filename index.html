Good to Know

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Mosaic Hostel located?

Mosaic Hostel is at B1/85C, Assi Ghat Road, Anandbagh, Varanasi — just steps from Assi Ghat, in the heart of the city's most livable ghat neighbourhood.

How much does it cost to stay?

Dorm beds start from ₹499 per night and private rooms from ₹1,500 per night. Book direct on our booking page for the best available rates.

What are the check-in and check-out times?

Check-in is from 1:00 PM and check-out is by 10:30 AM. Reception is staffed 24 hours, so late arrivals are welcome — just let us know your arrival time.

Do you have female-only dorms?

Yes. Alongside our mixed dorms, we offer 6-bed and 4-bed female-only dorms with the same amenities, for solo female travellers who prefer them.

How far is the hostel from the railway station and airport?

Varanasi Junction is roughly 15–20 minutes away and Lal Bahadur Shastri Airport about 45 minutes. See our transfer guide for routes and fares.

What amenities are included?

Free high-speed WiFi, air conditioning, hot-water showers, lockers, a shared kitchen and a rooftop space — all included in your stay.
